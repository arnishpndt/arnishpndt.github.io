In person, in Vancouver

Coaching online. Community for life.

Coaching runs online across Canada and the US. The immersions and the annual mastermind happen in person in Vancouver.

Summit

The Vancouver immersion

Three days and two nights at the Fairmont Pacific Rim in your first 30 days. A private story download with Jamie, spa time, an alcohol-free dinner, and a written rebuild blueprint and 90-day plan to take home.

Annual

The Vancouver mastermind

Two days with clients and graduates: a keynote from Jamie, guest speakers with their own recovery stories, small-group hot seats, and graduation recognition. Alcohol-free, and nothing is recorded without consent.

Year-round

Cohorts and community

Group cohorts of 8 to 12 start each quarter and stay together for the full year. Monthly virtual masterminds, regional meetups, and a private app where you can go by a first name or alias.

Coming from outside Canada? We check travel eligibility when you enroll, not the week before you fly.

Who it’s for

Ready for a full year of work.

The program serves people who are ready to commit and stable enough to do the work. If you need clinical care first, we’ll help you get it, then invite you back.

A good fit

  • Founders, executives, professionals, athletes and high earners
  • Ready to commit for 12 months and involve at least one trusted person
  • Stable enough to do the work, with no medical withdrawal care needed
  • Finishing residential or outpatient treatment and wanting a year of structured support after

Care first, then coaching

  • You need medical withdrawal care
  • You use non-prescribed opioids and aren’t yet working with a prescriber
  • Current suicidal intent, or a recent suicide attempt or overdose
  • Untreated psychosis or mania, or an active eating disorder
  • A court- or employer-mandated treatment requirement (coaching can run alongside it, but can’t replace it)

“Not yet” isn’t “no.” You can re-apply after 90 days, or sooner with a note from your clinician.

Need help right now?

  • Emergency (US & Canada): 911
  • Suicide crisis (US & Canada): call or text 988
  • US — National Problem Gambling Helpline: 1-800-MY-RESET (1-800-697-3738)
  • BC — Gambling Support BC, 24/7: 1-888-795-6111
  • US — SAMHSA National Helpline, 24/7: 1-800-662-4357
  • Using alone? US: Never Use Alone 800-484-3731 · Canada: National Overdose Response Service 1-888-688-6677

Still Standing 365 is coaching and mentorship, not medical treatment. In crisis? Call or text 988 in the US and Canada, or call 911.

It is not medical care, detox, psychiatric treatment or therapy, and it works alongside licensed care, never in place of it. Prescribed medications are part of recovery; coaches never advise starting, stopping or changing one.

Coaching runs online across Canada and the US.

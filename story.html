My story

I lost everything. Twice. Nobody knew.

I built a company from nothing. Made payroll. Showed up to dinner. Came apart in private.

I run National Equipment Supply. From the outside I was fine.

I was also 325 pounds, blackout drunk every weekend, and gambling in secret. I told myself next Monday would fix it. It didn’t.

Willpower was never the problem. Carrying it alone was.

Nobody saved me. The answer was me — but I didn’t find it alone.

You come to a point in your life where willpower isn’t enough, and you finally face your why: why you want out, why it matters, who you’re doing it for.

That day I stopped hiding, let the right people in, and everything started to move.

When I was in it, I couldn’t find a room full of men like me: winning on paper, losing in private.

So I built one. I’m not a guru and I’m not your therapist. I’m your guide.

My story

I lost everything. Twice. Nobody knew.

I built a company from nothing. I made payroll, showed up to dinner, and came apart in private. This is the part nobody saw.

I run National Equipment Supply. I built it from nothing.

From the outside, I was fine. Better than fine. People came to me for advice. I was the guy who had it handled.

I was also 325 pounds. Blackout drunk every weekend. Watching the scale climb and ignoring every warning my body sent me.

I gambled. I hid it. I told myself next Monday would fix it. It didn’t.

Willpower was never the problem. Carrying it alone was.

The lie that cost me the most

The highs convinced me I was different. That the discipline I had at work would handle the rest of my life too. It doesn’t. That belief is the most expensive thing I’ve ever owned.

The lows are the kind you carry alone, because saying them out loud would collapse the life everyone thinks you have.

Getting on board with myself

Nobody saved me. That’s the truth. The answer was me. But I didn’t find it alone. The day I stopped hiding and let the right people in, everything started to move.

I’m on the other side now. The company. The body. Nights I don’t have to hide. I’m not finished, and I haven’t forgotten what it cost.

Why I built Still Standing

When I was in it, I couldn’t find a single room full of men like me. Men who were winning on paper and losing in private. Men who still had another level in them.

So I built one. I’m not a guru and I’m not your therapist. I’m your guide. I’ve walked this road, and I’ll walk it with you.

  • US — SAMHSA National Helpline, 24/7: 1-800-662-4357
  • Using alone? US: Never Use Alone 800-484-3731 · Canada: National Overdose Response Service 1-888-688-6677

Still Standing 365 is coaching and mentorship, not medical treatment. In crisis? Call or text 988 in the US and Canada, or call 911.

It is not medical care, detox, psychiatric treatment or therapy, and it works alongside licensed care, never in place of it. Prescribed medications are part of recovery; coaches never advise starting, stopping or changing one.

Coaching runs online across Canada and the US.

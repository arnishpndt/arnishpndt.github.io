Back to the questions
What happens after I apply?

A short application covers where you are, your safety, your goals and your support system. Then a free 30 to 45 minute discovery call to confirm fit and explain what coaching is and isn’t. You’ll get an answer within 48 hours. No countdown timers, no “last spot” claims, no same-call deadlines.

Is this rehab or therapy?

No. Still Standing 365 is coaching and mentorship led by someone who has lived it. It isn’t medical care, detox, psychiatric treatment or therapy. It works alongside licensed care, never in place of it, and when part of your plan calls for something more, we have the right people for it.

Can I keep taking my medication?

Yes. Prescribed medications are part of recovery, including medications for opioid or alcohol use disorder. Coaches never advise you to start, stop or change a medication. That stays between you and your prescriber.

What if I slip?

A slip doesn’t end the program. It resets the plan, not the person. You reach out, we work through it together, and the plan gets stronger.

What if I need treatment during the year?

Safety comes first. If you need more than coaching, we help you get it. You can pause for up to 3 months for treatment or medical care, and your end date moves with you, so you don’t lose your year.

Is it private?

Yes. No public member lists, and what’s shared in the group stays in the group. In the community app you can use a first name or an alias. The only exceptions are the ones the law sets: when someone’s life is at immediate risk, when a child may be at risk, or when a court order requires it. You’ll see these in writing before you start.

Where is it based?

Vancouver, British Columbia. Coaching runs online for clients across Canada and the US, and other countries case by case. The immersions and the annual mastermind happen in person in Vancouver.

What if I’m not a fit yet?

Some people need clinical care before coaching can help. If that’s you, we’ll share crisis resources and a referral, and invite you back. You can re-apply after 90 days, or sooner with a note from your clinician.

What if I’m still successful?

That’s exactly who this is for. Men with a lot to lose who want even more to gain.

I travel and run a business. Will this work for me?

Yes. Coaching runs online, so you can do this from anywhere. Daily check-ins happen in the app, and sessions work around your schedule.

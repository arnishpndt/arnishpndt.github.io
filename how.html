A free call, an honest sit-down, and a 12-month plan you actually follow. Here’s exactly what happens, and the road you’ll walk.

How it works

From losing control to standing tall in 3 steps.

1

Book a free call

Start with a short, private application about where you are and where you want to be. Then a 30 to 45 minute discovery call. No pitch. No judgment.

2

Sit down & evaluate

We look at all of it together: the habit, the money, the body, the people at home. You get an honest answer on fit within 48 hours.

3

Plan & execute

Onboarding week sets up your safety plan, first session and personal 90-day plan. Then 12 months of reps, with people who hold you to it.

The 12 months

One year. Four 90-day phases.

Lasting change comes from 365 days of reps. Every client follows the same curriculum, with a review at months 3, 6, 9 and 12.

Phase 01 · Months 1–3

Stabilize

  • Your safety plan and urge plan
  • Telling one trusted person
  • Trigger map and replacement routines
  • Sleep, training, nutrition, daily routines
Phase 02 · Months 4–6

Understand

  • The pattern behind the addiction
  • Shame, identity and self-talk
  • Stress, boredom, loneliness and anger, without escaping
Phase 03 · Months 7–9

Rebuild

  • Debt plan and financial transparency
  • Repair conversations and boundaries
  • Business and career goals, channelled in healthy ways
Phase 04 · Months 10–12

Lead

  • Leading at work and at home
  • Giving back to a newer member
  • Your lifelong relapse-prevention plan and graduation

Every week

Your 1:1 or group session, a daily check-in in the app (urge rating, wins, habit tracker), one workbook assignment, and a call or message with your accountability partner.

Milestones

30 days, 90 days, 180 days, 270 days, and graduation at 365, with recognition at the next Vancouver mastermind.

If you slip

A slip doesn’t end the program. It resets the plan, not the person.

The coaching

This isn’t rehab. It’s coaching for men who lead.

No labels. No shame. No sitting in a circle talking about how powerless you are. You’re not powerless. You’ve been operating without the right system and the right people.

It’s coaching and mentorship from someone who has lived it, not medical care, detox or therapy. It works alongside your doctor or counsellor, never in place of them. And when you need more than coaching, we help you get it and hold your place in the year.

Guide

A guide, not a guru

Jamie has walked this road. He knows where it turns, and he walks it with you.

Team

The right people

When part of the climb calls for something specific, we have the right people for it, and we coordinate with the care you already have.

Private

Discreet by design

No public member lists. What’s shared in the group stays in the group, and in the community app you can go by a first name or an alias.

The journey

Five stages. One comeback.

Every man who comes back walks the same road. Here’s the map, so you always know where you are and what comes next.

→Start with a free call
  1. 1

    The wake-up

    The moment you stop pretending it’s fine. Maybe it’s a number in the account, a look from your kid, or a morning you can’t remember. Something cracks, and you can’t un-see it.

  2. 2

    The decision

    Nobody can make this one for you. The answer is you. You choose yourself, apply, and say it out loud for the first time. That’s where the silence breaks.

  3. 3

    The reset

    We sit down and look at everything. Then we clear the noise: break the loop, put blocks on the apps and accounts, remove the triggers, and set a simple daily structure so you’re not running on willpower.

  4. 4

    The rebuild

    With the habit out of the driver’s seat, the real work starts. Confidence, body, money, home. Weekly coaching and the right people in your corner, so every part of your life starts pulling in the same direction.

  5. 5

    Still standing

    You lead from the front, at work and at home. Nothing to hide. And when the next man walks in the door, you’re the one who reaches back and pulls him up.
